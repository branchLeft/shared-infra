What is Ghost?

Ghost is publishing software that connects you directly with your readers, with no platform in between.

Publishing

A clean editor for articles, images and video, with your own design, sections and authors.2

Newsletters

Any article can go straight to your readers' inboxes, and readers sign up on your own site.3

Members and payments

Free and paid members, with subscriptions taken through your own Stripe account. Ghost takes no cut: you pay only Stripe's usual fees.3 We're planning to add an EU-based payments provider, better aligned with our values, soon after PublicPress launches.

The social web

People on federated social apps can follow your publication, and like, reply to or repost what you publish.4

Yours to keep

Your articles and your members list export whenever you want, in Ghost's own format.35

Where PublicPress comes in

Ghost is software, and someone has to run it. PublicPress runs it for you: the servers, upgrades, backups, mail and support, on independent infrastructure.

Open source, and source-available

It is open source, under the MIT licence,1 and developed by the Ghost Foundation, a non-profit organisation.2 Anyone can read the code, and nobody can take it away from you. All code for PublicPress is source-available under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0, so you know exactly what technology you're running on.

Upgrades and maintenance

Ghost's patch and minor releases go on automatically, and we handle major versions for you. You never touch a server.

Backups

Taken every night, encrypted with your site's own key, and kept in two places apart from your live site.

Reliability

Releases do not take your site offline and they roll back if anything goes wrong. Every site is isolated from every other, watched continuously. PublicPress system status is reported on a public page in real-time, and your site's status is reported in the client portal.

Newsletters, sent your way

Choose how your newsletters reach your readers:

  • branchLeft's sovereign mail server. Built in. Our own server, on our own independent infrastructure, with nothing else to sign up to.
  • Sweego, an EU-based email provider. Higher-reliability mail delivery from an established sender, without leaving the EU, at additional cost.
  • Your own Mailgun plan. Ghost's newsletters were designed around Mailgun, so if you already have a plan you can keep it.

Why three? Each makes a different trade between independence, delivery reliability and what you already use, and the right one depends on your newsroom.

Integrations

We will build first-class support for UK-based technologies, with no extra charge for the integration. You are free to connect anything you like through Ghost's integrations API. And if you need something that does not exist yet, we can build bespoke integrations and code for you.

Content safety

Every uploaded image is scanned and signed before it can appear on your site. Images are checked for harmful content using Arachnid (and other methods TBD), to protect the security of our platform and to promote a safer internet. Automated moderation for comments (and optionally article text) follows after launch.

Respect for your readers

No non-essential cookies on a default site, so no consent banner between your readers and your journalism. Ghost's analytics is off by default for your readers' privacy, and you can turn it on for your site at no extra cost.

A client portal to run it from

Your domain, plan, billing, configuration... all in one place.

Freedom to leave

Ghost's own export gives you your content, members and settings whenever you want them.

How PublicPress is built

Each layer, from your readers down to the electricity, what it does and why it is there.

All of it is run for independent journalism. Any surplus goes back in.

Your readers and members

What: your sign-up, member and supporter records.

Why: they belong to you, and Ghost lets you export your members and posts whenever you want.

Apps, discovery, supporter tools

What: shared tools being built across the sector by others, and we will likely build them too.

Why: Ghost's APIs and webhooks are enabled on every site, so these connect with nothing for you to install or run.

Your Ghost site

What: publishing, newsletters, paid memberships, your domain.

Why: every Ghost feature, working as Ghost intended, including the two that take extra work to run. Ghost's analytics is off by default for your readers' privacy, and you can turn it on for your site at no extra cost. The Network lets people follow and reply to your publication from the wider social web. Paid memberships use your own Stripe account, because Ghost requires it, and we plan to add an EU-based payments provider soon after launch.

PublicPress

What: upgrades, backups, mail, monitoring, content safety, isolation, support and your portal.

Why: so nobody in your newsroom has to be a systems administrator. The source is public for anyone to read, and the platform is continually hardened and attack-tested in-house.

Independent infrastructure

What: hosting, storage, database and mail in the EU, with no AWS, Google Cloud or Azure.

Why: your journalism should not depend on the companies it may need to report on. Your live site runs in German data centres that Hetzner powers from renewable sources. So that your backups survive even if Hetzner has a catastrophe, a second copy goes to OVHcloud in London, locked so that nobody, including us, can change or delete it for 14 days. One exception to residency: our image check sends Arachnid a fingerprint of an image, never the image itself, and the fingerprint cannot be used to reconstruct an image.

Why we are doing this

Nobody in a newsroom should have to be a systems administrator.

The average independent news outlet in the UK has about two full-time-equivalent staff and £62,000 a year coming in (PINF Index 2024, reported by Press Gazette). Your website, mailing list and membership system still have to work every day, and between them they hold the most valuable thing you have: a direct relationship with your readers.

PublicPress exists to support the mission of independent journalists and outlets, not to generate profit. Any surplus goes back into the platform and the people who run it.

Removing the barrier to entry

Most newsrooms have no technical team. PublicPress gives you a modern, open publishing stack, managed by experts aligned with the mission to bring high-quality reporting on the topics that matter most, from hyper-local issues to journalism that speaks truth to power.

What we put back

The source is public, and what we learn running this goes back to the sector. We keep building after launch, and are refining a roadmap that tackles the unique problems faced in the UK by independent journalists and newsrooms.
